# SurakṣāMārga.ai

## Subject
**5G-Enabled AI Navigation System for Women Safety**  
A proactive cartographic application built exclusively around women's safety, fundamentally rethinking how urban navigation routing operates.

---

## Project Description
Urban mobility systems predominantly focus on journey speed, leaving critical vulnerabilities unaddressed for pedestrians—especially women traveling alone at night. Current commercial mapping applications like Google Maps rely exclusively on a "fastest route" philosophy. This inherently ignores safety dimensions, frequently forcing women into unsafe shortcuts, desolate backstreets, and poorly lit alleys without any preemptive warning.

**SurakṣāMārga.ai** solves this massive systemic failure. This project is a proactive navigation platform that maps paths based on the safest traversal from Point A to Point B. Breaking away from traditional routing, the architecture employs an AI-based safe routing engine that evaluates thousands of real geographic crime records (such as assault, harassment, and theft) to penalize dangerous roads. By bridging geospatial routing with real-world danger prevention, the system actively navigates women away from high-threat zones.

Furthermore, capitalizing on 5G edge computing, the system features a natively embedded SOS Emergency protocol. This eliminates high-latency data transmission buffers, instantly triggering distress beacons, live location tracking, and rapid responder alerts. Ultimately, SurakṣāMārga.ai transforms navigation from a convenience tool into a real-world safety infrastructure.

---

## Core Goals
The mission of SurakṣāMārga.ai is to ensure women can travel securely across metropolitan spaces. The technical goals include:

1. **Safety prioritized over speed:** Algorithmically enforcing safety constraints over proximity or travel time.
2. **Women-Centric Risk Handling:** Utilizing specialized crime severity indexing where crimes uniquely threatening to women receive maximum analytical penalization.
3. **Temporal Intelligence:** Automatically deploying aggressive avoidance multipliers for night-time navigation, redirecting mapped paths toward well-lit thoroughfares.
4. **Hardware-Level Responsiveness:** Bypassing standard 4G ceilings with 5G edge-computing nodes to guarantee sub-50ms AI computations and instantaneous distress location loops.
5. **Real-time Situational Awareness:** Equipping the interface with a dynamic crime heat-map to give transparent visual context to safety hazards.

---

## Future Scope (Scalability)
While current iterations are highly effective on the Bengaluru dataset, the underlying technical pipeline is engineered for extreme data scalability. 

- **Predictive AI heat-mapping:** Integrating high-volume, real-time nodal signals to predict threats dynamically.
- **National Expansion:** Scaling the AI algorithms to ingest datasets from all major national cities.
- **Node Integration:** Establishing automated alert pipelines connecting directly to government and police response centers.
